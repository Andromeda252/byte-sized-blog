Following a solid week of gaming showcases from Sony, Microsoft, and Summer Game Fest, Nintendo is throwing their hat into the ring. They announced this morning that the long awaited and highly anticipated first full-fledged Nintendo Direct of 2026 will be going down tomorrow at 10AM EST. The presetation is expected to last about 50 minutes, longer than a typical Direct, but shorter than last years September Direct, and will primarily focus on fleshing out the lineup for the remainder of 2026. Afterwards, we'll recieve about an hour and a half of Nintendo Treehouse Live coverage, presumably going into further detail on all the new announcements.
