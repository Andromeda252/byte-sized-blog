Hi, hello! If you're reading this, you've found my personal corner of the internet! Welcome!

This is The Byte-Sized Blog, a gaming blog focused on celebrating the games I love and am passionate about. If you're curious about exactly what this place is and what to expect, read on, as I'll try to provide as much information as possible!

# Who Am I, and Why Did I Start This Blog?

Online, I go by the name Andromeda. As of the creation of this blog, I am a 23 year old recent college graduate currently trying to find work in Computer Science. I wanted to build something from scratch to demonstrate what I can do, so I decided to expand upon one of my favorite school projects that has been fermenting in the back of my mind for years: a gaming blog focused on short-form, optimistic and enthusiatic gaming content. I took that idea and built this blog from the ground up, realizing that concept as a real website. And while the whole 'short-form' idea has kinda faded as time has gone on (more on that in the next section), everything else about that original idea has translated to this final product, the site on which you are currently reading this article.

So, that's how The Byte Sized-Blog came to be, but why? What drove me to create a gaming blog of all things as my first major solo project? Well, I've always wanted to do some kind of content creation. I'm the kind of person that regularly talks to myself about gaming topics that interest me as if an audience was listening, but of course, those thoughts have rarely actually left my head. That is what this blog is for, to provide an outlet for me to finally be able to put all those thoughts swirling around in my head out into the world.
