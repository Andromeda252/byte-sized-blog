It's that time of year again!

As September rapidly approaches, Nintendo fans such as myself have begun the annual ritual of discussing whne we're likely to see a new Nintendo Direct. While nothing is completely confirmed, it certainly does feel like a matter of when versus if. Since the start of the Switch era, there has been a major general September Direct every year except for two, 2020 and 2024, both having pretty good reasons for skipping out. We didn't get a single general Direct in 2020 due to a change in how Nintendo approached news during an uncertain period, and there was no general Direct in September of 2024 simply because there really wasn't much left to announce until the Switch 2 news cycle ramped up. And in both cases, we still got something, with the Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary Direct in 2020, and an Indie World + Partner Showcase combo at the tailend of August in 2024, so basically a September Direct.

When you consider that as well as the fact that we still know next to nothing about the Ocarina of Time remake, which is supposed to release in the next 4 months, plus the thin lineup of currently announced 2027 titles, and it becomes pretty clear that a big Direct is almost certainly right around the corner.

So, I wanted to talk about this hypothetical Direct, both regarding what I expect to be there and what I hope to see. I'll be splitting this up into three categories:

- Predictions: Things that I feel fairly confident could be in this Direct.
- Hopes: Things I don't anticipate being there, but are still somewhat realistic and I would love to be proven wrong.
- Pipe Dreams: Things I just generally really want that *probably* aren't gonna happen.

I think this set up covers a wide spectrum of topics ranging from things that are pretty much a lock to things that are highly unlikely but I really want anyway.

This'll be a long one, so buckle up, as I have a lot to predict and a lot to say.

# Predictions

- ## Ocarina of Time Remake Gameplay and Release Date Revealed, Launching in the First Half of November

Well DUH.

Let's start with a prediction that's about as likely as the Sun coming up tomorrow. Ocarina of Time remake is Nintendo's big blockbuster this holiday, so there's absolutely no chance that they don't show it off in great detail.

I see this Direct being formatted similarly to last year's September Direct, which was essentially a 20 minute Mario 40th Anniversary Direct and a 40 minute general Direct merged into one. I think we'll start off everything for Zelda's 40th Anniversary, perhaps cold opening with a trailer for OOT Remake that properly shows off the game before Eiji Aonuma goes into more detail.

As for the release date, I'm thinking the first half of November makes a lot of sense. Nintendo of all companies probably doesn't need to worry about GTAVI, and since that window has largely been vacated by games trying to get as far away from GTA as possible, it opens the door for Nintendo to waltz in with Ocarina of Time, allowing it to have the first half of November *allllll* to itself before GTA comes out. I originally was going to place it in December, until I realized there's another upcoming Switch 2 exclusive that I feel would be a better fit for December. I'll get to that shortly.

As for what I expect the game to be like? I have *NO* idea. I anticipate it will be more than a simple one-to-one remake in the same vein as Star Fox from earlier this year, but it's hard to predict exactly what the scope will be. Personally, I'd love for it to be the most ambitious remake Nintendo's ever done, setting a new standard for Nintendo remakes. But I just don't know if that's what this is going to be. Plus, I've always found the relatively short time between reveal and release for this remake to feel odd, especially if it were to be as ambitious as I hope.

There's a rumor from a while ago that's stuck with me for whatever reason that this could be a multi-part remake in the same vein as the FFVII remake, with this game we're getting this year covering the Young Link portion of the game. I keep thinking about this for some reason, and it's something I could honestly see. I think it has a lot to do with my mind being unable to comprehend the idea of a potetially massive game we've seen nothing of being just a couple months away.

Hopefully we won't have to wait much longer to learn exactly what this remake is, as it will almost certainly be in this potential Direct.

- ## Wario Land Returns in Early 2027 with a Samus Returns-esque Reimagining of Wario Land II

This is another prediction stemming from a rumor that’s been doing the rounds.

The leaker who correctly leaked both Rayman Legends Retold and Spyro: A Realm Beyond, both games I’m looking forward to, has claimed Nintendo has a new Wario game in development. I’ve been of the opinion for a while now that Wario Land’s return is inevitable, and I think we could finally see it in this upcoming Direct.

The Wario Land series has seen a lot of support on Nintendo Classics lately, with Wario World, VB Wario Land, and Super Mario Land 3: Wario Land being released on the service in the past year. Curiously, Wario Land II remains absent from the Game Boy library, and combining that information with the rumor of a new Wario game leads me to believe that Wario Land II could be getting a remake as a means of reviving the Wario Land series.

It would fall in line with a strategy Nintendo has had lately when it comes to reviving dormant franchises, starting with a remake of a classic title, and if that generates enough interest, eventually creating a new entry in that franchise. The most prominent example of this is Metroid Dread, which exists because Nintendo commissioned MercurySteam to remake Metroid II. They did such a good job that they gave MercurySteam the keys to make a brand new Metroid game that continued the story of the 2D saga. And with my previous prediction regarding Metroid Ravenous, I believe they will be the home of 2D Metroid for a long time to come. Another potential example is Star Fox, which saw a fantastic remake of Star Fox 64 developed by Velan Studios released earlier this year, and if it’s successful, could lead to a new era of the Star Fox franchise headed by Velan.

So, who would be developing this hypothetical Wario Land II remake? My first thought would be Good Feel, who made the most recent game in the series, Wario Land: Shake It on the Wii. However, they’ve released two games, Yoshi and the Mysterious Book and Princess Peach: Showtime, in the last few years. I can’t see them putting out a third game in such quick succession.

My second thought would be the 2D Mario team, but I highly suspect that they are working on a new Donkey Kong Country as their next project. Perhaps Next Level Games could be working on it, but they’re probably deep in development on Luigi’s Mansion 4 after the success of the third game.

So, my actual guess is that it will be a completely different studio we aren’t thinking of that will be making this potential Wario Land II remake. Perhaps it could be a studio that’s worked with Nintendo before, like how Velan did Mario Kart Live before eventually making the new Star Fox. Perhaps WayForward, who did the Advance Wars 1+2 remake a few years ago? Or it could be someone else entirely! That’s why I’m keeping this vague: it could be anyone!

As for why I specifically am predicting a Wario Land II reimagining, it ties into the lack of Wario Land II on Nintendo Classics. It would’ve made so much sense to add the second game at the same time as the first game back in July, but they didn’t. Perhaps there’s a reason for that.

My usage of the word “reimagining” means that I don’t expect this to be a one-to-one remake, but a game that takes the general design of Wario Land II, but modernizes and expands on it, much like Samus Returns, to the point that it almost feels like a new game.

As for a release date, I definitely see this as a Q1 release. Specifically, I’d place it in mid-February. I think this would make for a great early year game to play while we wait for the bigger stuff to arrive later in 2027.

- ## Kingdom Hearts Union X HD-2D Remake

I'm currently experiencing an immense level of hype for the Kingdom Hearts franchise after IV's surprise re-emergence in the last Nintendo Direct, and the big Coco trailer that debuted at D23 last month that confirmed IV would be coming out late next year. Add on the upcoming Collection that's finally bringing the series natively to a Nintendo platform, and my excitement for KH is at an all time high.

Now, news on KHIV in this Direct is probably unlikely considering Tetsuya Nomura said in a recent interview that the gap of new information for Kingdom Hearts IV following the D23 trailer will be wider this time. That all but rules out any KHIV news at this potential Direct, but I can dream, can't I?

However, that doesn't mean something else couldn't show up.

For context, over the past decade or so, the Kingdom Hearts series has relied heavily on mobile games to flesh out its lore. The problem with this, on top of critical lore being contained in games I and many others just weren't interested in playing, is that these mobile games are either unavailable or were cancelled. So, with their story elements seeming to have major significance in IV, I think you can see why this is a bit of a mess the series has found itself in. The stories required to fully understand what's happening in the next major game are either inaccessible through official means, or were never even released in the first place, in the case of the cancelled Missing Link mobile game.

There's been speculation that some of these stories could be told through the recently announced Kingdom Hearts anime series, but what I'd like to see is a full HD-2D reinterpretation of Union X, the most prominent of these mobile games, which tells the origin story of the KH universe.

At first, I thought that if this were to happen, it would be after IV, but there's a couple things I've learned that have changed that view a bit, and at the very least make me confident it will *eventually* happen.

I'm referring to Octopath Traveler 0 and Final Fantasy Resonance, both games I initially thought were just cool spinoffs, are actually reinterpretations of mobile games from those franchises. Octopath 0 reimagines Octopath Traveler: Champions of the Continent, and Final Fantasy Resonance reimagines Final Fantasy Brave Exvius. There's a trend here, one I could easily see Square extending to Kingdom Hearts. Plus, the recent Adventures of Elliot has proved that an action RPG can work in HD-2D, so why not?

There's also the fact that both of those games were revealed pretty shortly before their releases. Octopath was revealed in last year's July Partner Showcase, and released that December. Resonance was announced in the recent June Direct, and is releasing in October. So I could totally see something like a Union X HD-2D remake being announced and released shortly after.

I do feel fairly confident that something like this will happen eventually. I doubt it would be this Direct, but it would be a really cool surprise if it was. If it did happen in this Direct, I imagine it releasing in early-ish 2027 to coincide with the series' 25th anniversary.

Speaking of that 25th anniversary, we're almost certainly going to get some announcements when the anniversary rolls around next year, likely focusing on Kingdom Hearts IV and the anime. But, if not in a Direct like this, I could see that being the perfect place to announce something like this, giving it a Summer 2027 release date to tide us over that last little bit before KHIV releases.

All of this to say, this is something I really want to happen. Not only would it make an important part of the KH story more widely available, but I also just really want to see the series go back to more traditional spin-offs that expand the lore, rather than relegating side stories to free-to-play mobile gacha games with hours of filler content between major story beats.

Hopefully if anything good comes out of the cancellation of Missing Link, it'll be a return to traditional console spinoffs, leaving this era of annoyingly lore-important mobile games in the past. Because, like, surely Nomura can't be too happy about key (ha) pieces of the story he's trying to tell being completely inaccessible, right???

# Pipe Dreams

- ## POKEMON MYSTERY DUNGEON

It's been over 6 years since the Rescue Team remake. It's been nearly 11 years since the last original title. Both the Pokopia and Mega Dimension DLC announcements from last year's September Direct got my hopes up for a brief moment before they crashed back down to reality.

I've all but given up on actively hoping for this series' return... and yet, there's still a part of me, perhaps a naive part, that wants to believe...

Pokemon Mystery Dungeon is my favorite game series of all time. The second game in particular, Explorers of Time/Darkness/Sky, is my favorite game of all time. Few games have moved me the way these have. Few games have been as inspirational and influential on my life as these have. The world they're set in, the stories the they tell, the characters you meet, the beautiful and emotional music backing it all up, it all adds up to a criminally underrated and underappreciated series that is perhaps the first example I'd personally point to as to why games are art.

That's why, if I could have one gaming wish fulfilled, it would be a brand new entry in the Pokemon Mystery Dungeon series. Either that, or a remake of Explorers, in a similar vein to the Rescue Team remake. I would take any kind of sign that this series hasn't been cast aside into the pile of Pokemon spinoff series that are never to be touched again.

I just find there to be nothing quite like these games and the emotions they can evoke. I've always liked the actual Pokemon themselves more than any other attribute of the series, so being cast into the role of one and getting to experience a world belonging solely to Pokemon is way more appealing to me than the average core Pokemon RPG. I still love those games, but they don't fully represent what I love most about the series. In this sense, Pokopia scratched that itch for a Pokemon-centric game, but it far from fills that PMD-shaped hole in my heart.

Do I think it will happen in this Direct? No. Do I think I will happen eventually? I don't know. But no matter how long I have to wait, even if nothing ever comes to fruition, there will always be some part of me that continues to hope, to believe that someday, the Morning Sun shall shine on this series once again.

- ## Starfy Revival

I don't know if you've noticed, but in the past 5 or 6 years, Nintendo's brought back a lot of previously dormant franchises. From noteworthy IP that have or could become staples, such as Metroid and more recently Star Fox, beloved spin-offs like Pokemon Snap, Mario & Luigi, and Kirby Air Ride coming back with new entries, to niche and forgotten, yet beloved series like Advance Wars, Another Code, Endless Ocean, Rhythm Heaven and Tomodachi Life returning. Many casual series from the DS and Wii era have made a comeback at some point, with multiple continuations of the Wii Sports series, plus new Big Brain Academy and Clubhouse Games titles. Heck, Famicom Detective Club not only saw its two entries remade and finally localized, but we also got a brand new Famicom Detective Club game that was promoted with one of the most ingenious marketing stunts I've ever seen from Nintendo.

So, with seemingly any dormant Nintendo franchise eligible for a revival, there's one series close to my heart that I want to see more than any other... besides Pokemon Mystery Dungeon, of course. That being The Legendary Starfy.

This was a decently active series in Japan on the GBA and DS, but we only ever got the last game here in the West, just titled The Legendary Starfy. That game was a key component of my childhood DS collection, alongside the likes of Mario Kart DS, New Super Mario Bros, and Super Mario 64 DS. I've always loved this game and its unique gameplay, being a platformer focused on underwater traversal, along with its charming cast of characters, memorable soundtrack, and surprisingly compelling plot. I also remember it for containing the source of one of biggest childhood frustrations, with the final phase of the final boss requiring you to mash the Y Button more than my young fingers were capable of. I couldn't beat the game for *YEARS* because of that, and only managed to see the ending after getting help from a friend. Nowadays it's something I can do no problem, but back then, it was one of the biggest insurmountable walls of any game I played.

All that to say, The Legendary Starfy means a lot to me, so ever since I learned there were numerous other games in the series that had only released in Japan, I've been longing for them to be brought stateside.

It *kinda* happened a few years ago when the first three games were released on GBA - Nintendo Classics internationally, but they're untranslated. And since I can't read Japanese, I have no idea what's going on.

So, I would love to see Nintendo bring this series back with full remakes of these classic games, fully translated for the rest of the world. A collection with remakes of multiple games, in the vein of Advance Wars 1+2 and Another Code Recollection, would be ideal. They don't have to go crazy with these remakes either, something akin to what they did with Advance Wars would more than suffice. I just want to play these games officially more than anything else, and perhaps someday, it could lead to a brand new Starfy game!

Do I expect this to happen? Mmmm... maybe eventually, but probably not in this Direct. Though, with how many franchises Nintendo has brought back, and with them releasing the original games untranslated on Nintendo Classics worldwide, I do feel like it's only a matter of time before Nintendo decides to give the prince of Pufftop another crack.
