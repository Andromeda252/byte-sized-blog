Nintendo Classics, formerly known as just 'Nintendo Switch Online', was kind of a laughing stock when it was first introduced alongside the NSO service in September of 2018. The lackluster selection of only 20 NES games which many of us had already played before was emblematic of the poorly received launch of Nintendo Switch Online.

I could go on about the painful first few years of Nintendo Classics before things finally started to turn around with the introduction of the Expansion Pack, but we'd be here all day. That's a discussion for another time.

Nowadays, what began as small library of 20 NES games has blossomed into an incredibly impressive lineup now spanning 8 different platforms, including the GameCube and freaking Virtual Boy of all things, which were never included on previous Virtual Console services.

But with how much Nintendo Classics has grown, it has me wondering, have we reached the peak? Or is there further room to expand the lineup?
