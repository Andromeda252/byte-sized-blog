I've been thinking about Kirby a lot lately, going down YouTube rabbit holes on the franchise's shockingly intricate lore and trying to make sure I'm all up to speed on the latest revelations, questions, and lingering mysteries heading into the newest entry in the series, Kirby and the World Beyond, next spring.

And since Kirby, and by extension, the upcoming release of World Beyond has been on my mind so much as of late, I'vee naturally been doing a lot of internal speculation as to what exactly we can expect from the game when it launches next year. The initial reveal trailer from last month's Nintendo Direct didn't give us a lot to chew on, only serving to show how it would be evolving the 3D gameplay from Forgotten Land into a more open space, and hinting at the game's premise of exploring a vast world beyond what seems to be a fake, Truman-show esque copy of Dream Land. Heck, even just saying that is little more than speculation at this point, as the trailer and accompanying material gives us so little in terms of concrete details, that it's currently near impossible to grasp what's actually happening, and what implications it may carry for the lore and greater Kirby universe.

So, that's not what I'm going to try to do today. There's already plenty of folks online more knowledgable about Kirby lore than I am that have dissected everything we've seen so far are presented their own conclusions based on that limited information. So instead, I'd like to speculate about what the actual *game* is going to look like, what shape it's going to take, the structure of its progression, existing games it could be comparable to, etc.

Basically, while it's clear that World Beyond will be a much more open 3D platformer than the linear obstacle course design of Forgotten Land, it's not yet completely clear the exact degree of open-ness this game is aiming for. Is it a full open world game, akin to a larger-scale Bowser's Fury? Or is it a collection of individual open zones, akin to the sandbox 3D Marios like 64 and Odyssey, as well as Sonic Frontiers?

Or, is it something somewhere in the middle? Not fully open world in the traditional sense, but not sectioned off like the sandbox platformers of the past. That is the argument I'll be posing to you today, as I have a pretty clear vision for how I think Kirby and the World Beyond will be structured, and I can't quite think of any other games to directly compare it to.

This post was inspired by a conversation I had at work with a co-worker about the game and what exactly it'll end up looking like, whether it'll lean open world or open zone. It was then that my theory for how World Beyond will be structured fully came together, and I'd like to present that theory to you and provide the evidence that led me to that conclusion. Of course, this is all just fun speculation on a game we still know very little about, so in a few months time when we get more information, I could end up looking back at this and realizing that I was *COMPLETELY* wrong. I just thought it would be fun to take what little we've seen so far and try to piece together my own conclusions as to what the game's structure and progression might end up looking like.

So, allow me to start by laying out my theory. As I mentioned earlier, I currently believe that Kirby and the World Beyond will be neither a fully open world platformer, nor an open zone collectathon, but rather something that blurs the line between the two. In short, I see this game being an open zone sandbox platformer in a completely interconnected world, with the game's areas all being connected via a central 'hub' area.
