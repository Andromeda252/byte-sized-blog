At long last, it finally happened. Perhaps the most requested addition to Nintendo Music has finally arrived tonight, just a few days ahead of Mario Kart World's first anniversary.
            
## [insert subheading here]
            
However, there is a catch in that this is just the beginning of the soundtracks roll out. Currently, the main music for the game's main tracks and battle courses is all available, as well as the Free Roam remixes hailing from Super Mario Kart. But the remainder of the game's Free Roam music, which makes up the bulk of the soundtrack, is planned to be added over time. A bit annoying, but a compromise I'm willing to take just to get some of this game's incredible music readily available to listen to. You'll be able to catch me getting unreasonably hyped everytime a new wave of tracks drops, I can tell you that.

Additionally, the Nintendo Music app recieved a somewhat substantial update, bringing it to version 1.6. The app now supports CarPlay and Siri on Apple devices, and the service can even be used on your computer through a web browser now! There's also a new 'My Mix' playlist that refreshs daily and is based on your listening history, something I know I'll get a lot of usage out of.

It makes me very happy to see one of my favorite Nintendo services continue to improve. Now, if anyone needs me, I'll be listening to Whistlestop Summit on loop.
