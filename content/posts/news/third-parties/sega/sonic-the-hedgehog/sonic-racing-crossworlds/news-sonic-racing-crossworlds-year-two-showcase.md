Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds was one of my favorite games of last year. I knew that would end up being the case from the moment I learned that it would be bringing my beloved Babylon Rogues back to prominence after years spent languishing in irrelevancy. Just goes to show how easy it is to win me over simply by including a playable bird character in your game, something [Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity can attest to.](https://www.the-byte-sized-blog.com/posts/news-hyrule-warriors-age-of-calamity-de-announced)

Though it certainly helps that it's just a flat out amazing racing game. It's easily my favorite non-Mario Kart kart racing game of all time, and in many ways I prefer it to Mario Kart World. Now, don't get it twisted, I love both games, but CrossWorlds just feels tighter and more refined in many ways while also bringing fresh ideas to the table, as opposed to Mario Kart World, which swung for the fences by trying something new and didn't completely stick the landing, despite still being a great game.

But one area in which CrossWorlds has undeniably beaten World in is post-launch support. While Mario Kart World has left us pondering about potential DLC plans for well over a year, CrossWorlds came right out of the starting gate with a full year of content planned, between free characters hailing from SEGA's IP catalog, paid DLC that brought crossover characters and courses from third party franchises and properties that even went beyond gaming, and regular in-game events, updates, and changes to ensure there was almost always a reason to go back to the game.

The game has grown a ton since it launched last September, and yet, it appears the past year was only the beginning, as the game is gearing up for a *MASSIVE* second year.

Last Friday, SEGA premiered the Year Two Showcase for Sonic Racing CrossWorlds, which, well, showcased what to expect from the game's second year of support. In short: you can expect a lot. From the continued roll out of both free and paid DLC, new ways to play, and potentially game-changing upgrades to core mechanics, there's a lot to go over. So, I'm not gonna waste any more time and jump right into what most people are likely most interested in: Year Two's Season Pass.

Joining the previously confirmed Godzilla and EVANGELION packs are Sanrio/Hello Kitty, Final Fantasy VII, Persona, and classic SEGA themed packs, creating a fascinating lineup that covers a broad spectrum of pop culture, spanning video games, anime, cinema, and even SEGA's own catalog this time around. You can view more detailed information about each pack below:

- ### Wave 1: Godzilla Pack - November 3rd, 2026
- Course: Godzilla Escape (It's literally Godzilla x City Escape. Actually brilliant)
- Machines: Godzilla Cruiser, King Ghidorah Coupe, Mothra Surf

- ### Wave 2: EVANGELION Pack - January 2027

- Characters: Rei Ayanami, Asuka Shikinami Langley

- Course: SEGALAND (close enough, welcome back Race of AGES)

Between SEGALAND and Persona, I definitely wasn't expecting to see SEGA's own IP represented in the paid DLC. Prior precedent from the game's first year had the free content focused on characters from SEGA franchises, and the Season Pass focused entirely on third party crossovers. It was a really cool surprise, though, as even with the inclusion of Joker as a free DLC character early on in the game's life, I found myself wondering what a Persona-themed track would look like. Seems we'll get to find out after all!

Overall, I'm pretty satisfied with this lineup. It feels much more varied than the first Season Pass, which was half video game crossovers and half Nickelodeon crossovers. It didn't feel like the concept of these third party crossovers was meeting its full potential with such limited sources, but this time, we've got all kinds of franchises represented! Final Fantasy VII in particular was super exciting to me, as not only was it one of my predictions for Year Two's DLC, but I also just really wanted to see it and thought it made a ton of sense.

These packs will be rolling out over the next year or so, with the Godzilla pack kicking things off on the same day Year Two begins, which is November 3rd, 2026, by the way!

Free characters will also continue rolling out throughout Year Two, headlined by Bayonetta, who was previously announced a few weeks ago when the Year Two Showcase was initially announced. We got the confirmation of two more, as well, dubbed in the showcase as "SEGA fan-favorites the community has been clamoring for", which apparently includes Sonic in a McLaren F1 uniform. Huh. Didn't realize that was such a heavy fan request.

That was probably the only part of the showcase that underwhelmed me and made me go, "really?". I mean, I guess the machine he's coming with is kinda neat, and I get that the Sonic brand has been partnering with F1 a lot lately, so it's not like this is completely out of left field, but when you tell me you're about to reveal "characters fans have been clamoring for", I naturally set my expectations a whole lot higher than "Sonic but he's an F1 advertisement now".

I can't wait for "Shadow in a Five Guys" uniform to be the next character they reveal.

Jokes aside, the final free character revealed is Aigis from Persona 3, which is interesting considering they're doing a Persona DLC pack in the new Season Pass, so, why not just include her in that? Regardless, it is cool to see another Persona character, even if it doesn't do anything for me specifically.

The graphic showing the roadmap of DLC content reveals 5 additional free character slots to be revealed and released throughout Year Two, meaning we'll be getting a total of 8 free characters this time around. That's down from the 12 that were initially promised for Year One, which ultimately ended up being 13 with the package addition of Tangle and Whisper. There's still some noteworthy SEGA characters absent from the lineup, primarily Beat from Jet Set Radio, so hopefully these remaining slots will help to round out the roster, as well as provide some fun surprises!

Aside from the free and paid DLC roadmap, there's some more substantial additions and changes coming to the game for Year Two, including Duel Mode, a new one-on-one mode encouraging strategic racing by allowing you to see your opponents items and gadgets at all times. Additionally, the gadget system is being upgraded with the addition of a new 'Grand Master Plate', offering a seventh slot exclusively for one-slot gadgets, but that will augment the effects of any gadgets placed there. Add in 15 new gadgets, as well as more on the way in the future, and these upgrades to the gadget system seem like they'll further expand the versatility and creative potential of one of CrossWorlds' most unique elements!

Oh yeah, they're also making those really cool SEGA console inspired vehicles they posted as an April Fools joke real as bonus content included with Year Two's Season Pass! And if you buy both Season Pass's, you can get Super Shadow as a playable racer!

And if all that wasn't enough, on top of everything we've already covered, the game's price has now been ***PERMANENTLY*** slashed from $69.99 to $39.99 for the standard edition and from $89.99 to $59.99 for the Digital Deluxe Edition. There's also going to be a physical Year One Edition of the game, priced at $59.99, that includes all the Year One content as well!

To recap:
- ***Year Two begins on November 3rd, 2026, arriving alongside Duel Mode, the Grand Master Gadget Plate, 15 New Gadgets, Bayonetta, and the Godzilla DLC Pack!***
- Eight free DLC characters are currently planned for Year Two, with Bayonetta, McLaren Sonic, and Aigis being currently confirmed
- Year Two's Season Pass brings Godzilla, EVANGELION, Sanrio, Classic SEGA, Final Fantasy VII, and Persona to the game over the course of the next year
- Duel Mode, a new one-on-one mode with an emphasis on strategic, tactical racing, is being added
- The Gadget System is being upgraded with the Grand Master Plate, featuring a seventh gadget slot, as well as new gadgets continuing to arrive regularly
- Owners of Season Pass 2 also get SEGA console inspired machines, and owners of both season passes get Super Shadow as a bonus
- The game's price has been permanently slashed to $39.99, and a physical Year One Edition will be available

In short, between a lowered price and a stacked year of support ahead, there's never been a better time to jump into Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds! As someone who absolutely loves this game, I couldn't be happier to see it getting such healthy and substantial support. If you're a fan of kart racers and haven't picked this game up yet, go do it. I can't recommend it enough, and I'm super excited to keep playing it for another year to come!
