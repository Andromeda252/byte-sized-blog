To answer the question posed in one of the [earliest posts on this blog](https://www.the-byte-sized-blog.com/posts/news-metroid-ravenous-leak): yes, Metroid 6 ***HAD***, in fact, just leak!

One of the biggest announcements from last week's Nintendo Direct was the announcement of the 6th entry in the 2D Metroid saga, Metroid Ravenous. Seemingly once again being handled by the incredibly talented folks over at MercurySteam, Ravenous continues the story from its predecssor, Metroid Dread, this time focusing heavily on Samus's new Metroid instincts that were awakened towards the end of Dread and served crucial to defeating Ravenbeak and escaping from ZDR.

We only got a taste of what Samus's new powers were capable of in Dread, but now we get to explore them over the course of an entire game in what looks to be the most visceral and intense Metroid title to date!
