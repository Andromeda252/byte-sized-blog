An eShop demo for Yoshi and the Mysterious Book was made available today, allowing Switch 2 players that haven't taken the leap yet to try out Yoshi's latest adventure.

I highly recommend checking it out if you haven't played the game or are unsure about it. Having beaten the game's main story, I was personally really surprised by how much I ended up enjoying the game, but my recommendation of the full game comes with the caveat that this is not a traditional platformer and won't be for everyone. This demo now makes it easy to get a feel for what this game is, as it is kinda tricky to explain exactly what this game is without experiencing it for yourself, and decide whether or not it's for you.

If you've been on the fence about Mysterious Book, I urge you to give the demo a download and take it for a spin, you might just be surprised by its quality. Or perhaps you won't, and that's valid to, as like I said, it's not for everyone. But if it ends up being for you, then trust me, you're in for a fun experience.
