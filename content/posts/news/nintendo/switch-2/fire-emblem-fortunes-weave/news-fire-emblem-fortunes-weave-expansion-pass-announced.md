It's been about a month since the release of Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and this past Wednesday night, Nintendo announced that the game would be receiving a paid expansion pass, as has been tradition for the past few titles.

The DLC is split up into three waves of content releasing over time, with the first available now, the second due out this holiday, and the third slated for next year. Though, looking at the contents of the DLC, it's pretty clear that the first two waves are moreso smaller bonuses, with the bulk of the expansion coming in the third wave's story content.

The first wave contains a collection of weapons modelled after the Heroes' Relics from Three Houses, and was shadowdropped alongside the announcement. So, if you buy the $34.99 expansion pass right now, you can scoop these weapons up as we speak!

Wave 2 is slightly more interesting. Launching sometime this holiday season, this wave adds new outfits for the 4 Flame Lords and their allies, as well as the ability to enlist the gods Aurora, Smyrnos, and Jurah into your forces. Like I said, these first two waves are moreso just smaller bonuses.

However, the real meat of the package comes in Wave 3, which is due to arrive some time next year. Wave 3 offers the story content that most will be intersted in, and the form it's taking isn't surprising. The new story content will focus on the characters of Bertrand, Talimun, Anatolia, and Orchel, and if you've played the game, you're probably not surprised the DLC is going in that direction. Of all the characters in the game beside the four Flame Lords, these four characters are clearly among the most prominent, to the point where they even have statues along the road to Dagsion in the Prologue! And if you read the Developer Interview, you'd know that the original intention was to have 8 protragonists, with these four characters likely rounding out that total. While their stories likely won't be as in-depth as the four in the base game, I am interested in learning more about each of them. From what I've played so far (keep in mind I haven't gotten *super* far in the game yet), seeing more of Bertrand's story in particular is highly intriguing to me, and then there's the fact that Orchel is one of my favorite characters so far, so more content focused on her is a win!

I'm currently taking a bit of a break from Fortune's Weave to focus on Tears of the Kingdom, which I've been very motivated to play and make progress in lately, but I do intend to continue playing it and chipping away at its story. That said, I likely won't be grabbing this right away, but should I continue with the game and not let it fall by the wayside as I have with so many other RPGs and games in general, I will likely jump on this DLC at some point, when it makes the most sense for me!
