Oh hey, would you look at that, the thing I've been predicting would happen every month for the past few months in my Monthly Roundups finally happened. Nice.

Nintendo announced this morning that the next Indie World Showcase will be broadcast tomorrow, October 28th, 2026, at 7:00 AM PT and 10 AM ET, lasting approximately 15 minutes!

I honestly have no idea what to expect from this presentation, as I can't currently think of any particular indie games off the top of my head that I'm hoping come over to Switch/Switch 2. There is Expedition 33, which is kind of an indie game, but that feels more suited to a general Direct or partner showcase, so I wouldn't expect to see it here.

Regardless, I almost always come away from these with at least one new game to keep an eye on, or immediately go buy if it was a shadowdrop, so hopefully we see some neat stuff tomorrow! I'll be here to round it all up as soon as I'm able! Hopefully it won't end up taking all month like the Direct roundup did.

Be sure to check out the presentation [here](https://www.nintendo.com/us/indie-world/showcase/)!
