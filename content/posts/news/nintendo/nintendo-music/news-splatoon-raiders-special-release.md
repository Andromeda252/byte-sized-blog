Happy Nintendo Music...... Wednesday??? But on a Thursday??????

Yes that's right, we've got another Special Release on our hands, and, not to brag, but I saw this one coming from a mile away (please care I'm very proud of myself).

Ahem. Anyway.

So, yeah, Splatoon Raiders, the first ever Splatoon spin-off, launches today. And, as has become tradition for most new Nintendo releases, a Special Release arrived on Nintendo Music last night three hours before the game launched here on the east coast, bringing a select few tracks from the new game to the service early.

This time around, we've got a total of 9 tracks, including a pair by Deep Cut, the idol group from Splatoon 3 that are key characters in Raiders. Other than that, though, if you're up to date on your Splatoon band lore, you'll notice every other artist in this sampling, of which there are 5 over 6 tracks, are brand new to the series, which is exciting! We've got The Mechanic (which I assume refers to the player character), DJ Spinsalt, purines, DHA, and The Maverick. Based on the thumbnails, some of the artists, namely DJ Spinsalt and DHA, seem to be Salmonid artists, which hopefully indicates we will get that deeper dive into Salmonid culture and lore that I've been craving.

You can view the 9 tracks, which total 30 minutes of music, in the screenshot below.

![The 9 tracks included in Splatoon Raiders' Special Release, including two Deep Cut songs, and 5 brand new artists to the series!](/images/nintendo/switch-2/splatoon-raiders/splatoon-raiders-special-release-nm.png)

I've listened to them all, and there's some good stuff here, with Cartograffiti v2 and HubBubble being standouts. Though as a bird enthusiast, I must say that I'm incredibly interested in the title of Deep Cut's Avian Ascension, which seems to be the mysterious song we heard at the end of the Raiders Direct. I don't think there are any birds in Splatoon, so I'm very curious what the implications of that title could be. Not much longer to wait now...
