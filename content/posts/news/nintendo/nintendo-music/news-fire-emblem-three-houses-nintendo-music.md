Happy Nintendo Music Thursday!

We're about three weeks out from the highly anticipated release of the next Fire Emblem game, Fortune's Weave, and I gotta say, I'm starting to get a little excited.

Now's a good time to mention that I have placed a pre-order and intend to make it my first Fire Emblem game! I have no idea if it'll end up clicking with me, but I'm looking forward to finding out.

In the meantime, the highly-regarded Switch entry Three Houses has finally arrived on Nintendo Music. This is one I've quietly been wanting for a bit because, as I learned from Smash, its main theme, The Edge of Dawn, is incredible.

I also imagine that the game just generally has great music, as I've really enjoyed the Engage soundtrack since it arrived on the service last year. I'm looking forward to listening through it as we build towards the release of Fortune's Weave next month!
