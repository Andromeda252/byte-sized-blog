Happy incredibly belated Nintendo Music Thursday!

Last week’s drop takes us all the way back to the Game Boy with Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3, the very first Wario Land game.

Somehow, between the game's arrival on Nintendo Classics and the Mario Kart World remix hitting Nintendo Music, this is actually the third time I’ve covered something related to this game in the couple months since I started this blog. I just find it kinda funny that this, of all games, already has three posts under its tag at the time of writing.

Regardless, spanning 66 tracks and 1 hour and 5 minutes of music, this soundtrack’s arrival finally completes the Super Mario Land trilogy on Nintendo Music, while kicking off the Wario Land series… well, that would be the case, but this is actually the second Wario Land game on Nintendo Music, as Virtual Boy Wario Land was first. Somehow. Again, that’s just kinda funny.

The first Wario Land game has actually been on my immediate backlog for a few months now, since its addition to Nintendo Classics back in July. I’ve been wanting to play through all the Wario Land games for a while now, but was waiting for this game and Wario Land II to get added before doing so. While we’re still waiting for Wario Land II, this soundtrack drop actually gave me the kick in the tail I needed to finally start playing Wario Land 1.

So far, my thoughts are kinda mixed. Sometimes it feels like the enemies are hard to read in terms of where you can hit them without getting hurt, and, not to sound like an IGN reviewer, but I’ve found the early game to have just way too much water, especially for a game where underwater movement is very limited and not very fun outside of having one particular power up. Plus, the bosses I’ve fought so far seem to be near impossible once you’ve taken a hit and entered small form. Overall to sum up my early thoughts, I feel like there’s something with this game I’m missing, cause it simply isn’t clicking or grabbing me.

Even if it’s not my favorite, though, I’m willing to power through it to get to the rest of the series. From what I understand, the second game is where the format of the series really takes shape. Though I’ve still got a lot of Wario Land 1 left, so perhaps the game will grow on me as I progress further!

Anyways, Switch Sports Resort is coming out in a few days, so we may be able to expect a special release for it this week. Though, I also wouldn’t be too surprised if they skipped out on a special release for this title. We shall see in the coming days.
