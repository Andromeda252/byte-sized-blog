August 2026 was an incredibly quiet month for Nintendo, with no major new releases and very little in the way of announcements and news. Heck, easily the biggest news this month that I cared about didn't come from Nintendo at all, as you can probably tell from the thumbnail I used this time around.

I know I call this series "Nintendo Monthly Roundup", but in reality, it serves as a recap of the biggest news that I personally care about. It just so happens that most months, that is almost exclusively Nintendo related news. That isn't the case this month, but dang it, Kingdom Hearts IV *IS* releasing on Nintendo Switch 2, so it counts. It was either that, or Super Mario Sunshine as the headliner for this months roundup. I suppose there was the Fire Emblem Direct early in the month, but that's next month's headliner.

I'll be using a similar format to last month, but I'll be cutting the 'Events' section, as I feel like that didn't really add much. If there are any in-game events I feel are worth mentioning, I'll rope them into the 'Miscellaneous' section at the end.

With all that said, however, let's jump right into what *DID* happen in August as we prepare for what's likely to be a *massive* September.
