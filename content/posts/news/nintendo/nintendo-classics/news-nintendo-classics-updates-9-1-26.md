Oh you thought Nintendo was done after dropping two free Switch 2 enhancement updates in quick succession? Hahaha, nope!

A couple nights ago, at about the same time the 4.0.0 update for Mario Kart 8 Deluxe dropped, Nintendo also added three new titles to Nintendo Classics' NES library. Now, none of these games are all that earth-shattering for me personally, but it's neat to see them still finding new NES games to add nearly 8 years into the library's existence.

The games in question are Ikari Warriors, RC Pro-Am II, and Ninja Gaiden III. I don't have much to say on any of these titles, so I'll just quickly go over them real quick.

**Ikari Warriors** is an arcade game by SNK that made its way to the NES. It's a top down shooter game that pits you against hordes of enemies and seems to feature a co-op mode.

**RC Pro-Am II** is an isometric racing game from Rare (yes, *that* Rare), and is a sequel to, well, RC Pro-Am, obviously. The original game was previously added to the library, I believe as a part of the big Rare game drop we got during the February 2024 Partner Showcase. Y'know, the one where Japan got Mother 3 instead? Yeah, that one.

And finally, **Ninja Gaiden III: The Ancient Ship of Doom** should require little introduction. It's the third and final entry in the original NES trilogy of hard-as-nails action platformers following the journeys of Ryu Hayabusa. The first Ninja Gaiden was among the first titles added to the NES library after launch, but it took a while to get II, and now we finally have III to complete the trilogy!

Additionally, the Japanese app has a different title in place of RC Pro-Am II, being **Dragon Buster**, a Namco RPG. With the Switch consoles being region free, you can access it by creating a Japanese Nintendo Account and downloading the Famicom - Nintendo Classics app off of the Japanese eShop.

A pretty decent selection considering how slim the pickings have become at this point! RC Pro-Am II is probably the only one here I'm interested in trying, but regardless, I'm glad to see the NES library continuing to get updated from time to time!
