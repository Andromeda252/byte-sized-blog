Oh you thought Nintendo was done after dropping two free Switch 2 enhancement update's in quick succession? Hahaha, nope!

Last night, at about the same time the 4.0.0 update for Mario Kart 8 Deluxe dropped, Nintendo also added three new titles to Nintendo Classics' NES library. Now, none of these games are all that earth-shattering for me personally, but it's neat to see them still finding new NES games to add nearly 8 years into the library's existence.
