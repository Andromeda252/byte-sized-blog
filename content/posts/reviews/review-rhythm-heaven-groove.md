Byte Verdict: As a newcomer to the series, Rhythm Heaven Groove absolutely blew me away, and introduced me to what is now one of my new favorite franchises. With a $40 pricetag and a wealth of content, I can't recommend Rhythm Heaven Groove enough. If you're on the fence, give the free demo a spin to see if its for you!

Gameplay: Simplistic in the best way possible,and intoxicatingly addictive.
Visuals: Crisp, clean, and very pleasent on the eyes. The artstyle may be simple, but the franchise's first HD entry is easily the best its ever looked.
Soundtrack: Absolutely incredible, easily the star of the show. Filled to the brim with bangers throughout the entire rhythm game lineup.
Story: Not applicable. There's some unlockable lore for the various games and modes, but none of it is much more than fun flavor text.
Content: There's a ton to due. Between 80 solo games and 30 multiplayer games, theres a bunch of side modes, like Beatspell. Every stage has medals and perfects to earn as well. There's enough here to keep you busy for a long time if you're looking to master it.
