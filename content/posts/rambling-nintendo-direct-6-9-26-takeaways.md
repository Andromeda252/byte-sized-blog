Today's Nintendo Direct presentation would've seemed impossible a decade ago. To think that in that time we went from the depths of the Wii U era, when most companies barely acknowledged Nintendo's existence, to where we are now, it simply makes my head spin. And for me personally, no announcement exemplifies how far we've come moreso than the first new trailer for Kingdom Hearts 4 in 4 years.
            
## [insert subheading here]

After the PlayStation State of Play, Summer Game Fest, and Xbox Games Showcase had all come and gone with no sign of KH4, I was ready to accept that we probably wouldn't see anything of it until the franchise's 25th anniversary next Spring. Never once did the possibility of it appearing in the then-upcoming Nintendo Direct even remotely cross my mind, I never entertained it even as a flickering thought. As a result, I was sent into a state of sheer and utter shock and disbelief when that very thing that I never would've considered as a possibility, happened. Not only were we finally being graced with a new trailer for my most anticipated upcoming game, but it was happening somewhere I never could've anticipated.

No longer is Nintendo simply announcing ports of years old games in these Directs. Now, in this current, Switch 2 era, Nintendo is being entrusted by some of the biggest studios in gaming with the latest trailers for their biggest games. This isn't the first time this has happened either, as last year's September Direct featured a brand new trailer for Capcom's Resident Evil Requiem, that also served to announce the game's release on Switch 2. It's absolutely incredible to see, and signals a real shift in perception for Nintendo towards being a genuine gaming platform that stands right alongside PlayStation and Xbox, rather than lagging behind them.
