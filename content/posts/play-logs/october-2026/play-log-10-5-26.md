This week saw continued progress in the two games I've been primarily focused on with Tears of the Kingdom and Fortune's Weave, but a new challenger entered the ring this week as well.

Previously I've expressed my incredible excitment for the Kingdom Hearts Collection on Switch 2, and it finally launched this week! I grabbed it physically, marking my first physical third-party Switch 2 game, as well as my first Game-Key Card, a concept I was vehementely against to start the generation, but have since somewhat warmed up to, even if I still don't love it.

That's a completely different discussion, though, and I'd prefer not to bloat this thing with my thoughts on Game-Key Cards (perhaps another time), so for now, let's start talking about what I played this past week!

# Tetris 99 (NS on NS2)
- ### Played on Mon for ~15 mins

And here we have the obligatory participation in the latest Tetris 99 event. This time, theme was based on Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and considering how much I've been enjoying the game, I knew I had to put in the time to unlock this theme.

Not much else to say here other than how I'm impressed by how this game is still being supported upwards of 7 years after first releasing back in 2019, with no signs of slowing down anytime soon. Long live Tetris 99.
