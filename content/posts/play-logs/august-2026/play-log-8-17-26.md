# Rhythm Heaven Fever (Wii on Wii U VC)
- ### Played on Wednesday for

This started with me *really* wanting to play Remix 10 again. I'd been watching a ton of Remix 10 parody videos online that had helped me to become very familiar with the song and overall flow of the stage. So, I decided to boot the game up and try to get a "Suberb" on Remix 10 on my first try after having not played for a while.

Yeah, that went about as well as you'd expect.

But hey, after taking a couple more shots at it throughout the night, I would eventually get it, which felt really good! Remix 10 is an incredible finale to Fever, being very challenging and very satisfying to get down. And the ways in which they weaved each rhythm game's theme together into one continuos medley makes for one of the most memorable songs in the whole series.

Other than that, I chipped away at getting Suberbs in as many stages as I could, making great progress. I think I went from around the low 20s to the high 30s by the end of this play session, which I'm pretty happy with.

I think it's safe to say at this point that Fever is my favorite Rhythm Heaven game, despite Groove being my entry point and the game that made me fall in love with Rhythm Heaven in the first place. That's not to say my opinion on Groove has soured or anything, I just think Fever is that much better. They're all great games, though, and you can't go wrong with any entry in the Rhythm Heaven series.

# Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories (GBA SP)
- ### Played on Friday for ~15 mins

Betcha didn't expect this, huh? I do actually have a real GBA SP, which I got as a birthday present a few years ago. I don't use it a ton, but it's a cool novelty to whip it out every now and then, and I've compiled a pretty neat collection of games for it. Some of them, like Pokemon Emerald, Minish Cap, Super Circuit, and a pair of Super Mario Advance games, are reproduction cartridges. I didn't understand the difference between authentic and reproduction GBA cartridges back when I first got the system, but they're still neat to have. I also have a copy of the Mother 3 fan translation on a GBA cartridge, which, while also clearly not official, is really dang cool. If I ever get around to playing the Mother series, it's how I intend to play the third entry, barring an official translation ever happening (ha).

I do have a couple cartridges that I'm pretty sure are completely authentic, such as Namco Museum, and the topic of this segment, Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories. And with my Kingdom Hearts obsession back in full force in lieu of the massive KHIV news from last weekend, I thought it would be neat to pop it in and mess around with it for a bit.

Ever since I came into the Kingdom Hearts fandom and learned about this game, it's fascinated me. The fact that, one game into this series, they decided to make a spin-off on a completely different platform with very different gameplay, and have its story be fairly important to understanding what is going on at the start of KHII is just... well, it's one of the most Kingdom Hearts things of all time.

I've only ever played the PS2 remake, Re:Chain of Memories, and I'm not particularly fond of it- probably my least favorite game in the series- but that doesn't mean I'm any less compelled by the original GBA version.

It's a surprisingly ambitious and impressive game given the hardware its on. For one, the opening cutscene is a full-motion video that looks just like the cutscenes from the original PS2 game, crammed onto the GBA. It's actually kind of surreal to see this kind of footage running on a GBA's screen. As for the gameplay, they adapted the Kingdom Hearts gameplay to the GBA's much smaller number of buttons about as well as they could.

I pretty much only played up until the first save point, but it was enough to give me a taste of what is one of the most unique titles in the Kingdom Hearts franchise. Like I said, everything about this game is just so interesting to me. I imagine that there were a lot of kids out there that played Kingdom Hearts 1 and 2 and being incredibly confused on how Sora ended up in a memory-restoring sleep pod, unaware at the time that there was an entire other game out there that filled in that gap. I will never not find the decision to handle the early story in this way to be one of the decisions of all time.

But, like I said, if there's any franchise I'd expect that from, it's this one, and wouldn't have this crazy, fever dream of a series any other way.
