# Donkey Kong Bananza (NS2)
- ### Played on Monday for ~1 hr

So, I did it!

Last week I talked about my efforts to clear the final challenge of the DK Challenge, that being clearing the Final Rehearsal with no skills unlocked. I only spent about 45 minutes on it last Sunday night, but I quickly determined it was easily the hardest official challenge posed by Bananza in any shape or form, specifically because of the first area, covered in thorns, becoming a *nightmare* to traverse without certain skills like Ostrich Bananza's Glide.

So, on Monday night, I put some YouTube videos on in the background and got to grinding. As expected, most of my attempts ended in the starting thorny area, as it is by *FAR* the hardest area to complete without skills. But I eventually started to get more runs past the first area. From there, the biggest challenge was handling my nerves, as everything after that wasn't too bad. It's just that the sheer difficulty of the first area added a tension to the mix that made it feel like every move needed to be calculated, lest I risk having to do the beginning over again, which could end up taking a bit.

But, within an hour of play time, I had conquered the challenge, and *MAN* did it feel sweet.

![A screenshot of Donkey Kong Bananza, showing me at the end of the Final Rehearsal with no skills unlocked.](/images/nintendo/switch-2/donkey-kong-bananza/dk-challenge-rehearsal-challenge-clear-proof-1.png)

![Proof of my triumph over Donkey Kong Bananza and the DK Challenge's hardest challenge.](/images/nintendo/switch-2/donkey-kong-bananza/dk-challenge-rehearsal-challenge-clear-proof-2.jpg)

This was such a fun and satisfying challenge to overcome, and made for a great way to cap off the DK Challenge and secure my rights to that sweet, *sweet* physical card set that I want so much.

The key factor to my victory was absolutely a piece of equipment for either DK or Pauline (I can't remember who, I think it was Pauline) that gradually restored health when a Bananza was active. I used this during the 0 Skill Boss Rush Challenge as well, and it came in *super* clutch here towards the end of the Final Rehearsal, in the final 2D section. There's a pair of Banadium Melons right before the two final challenges of the stage, restoring your Bananergy, allowing you to use Bananzas again if your meter was depleted. Again, this came in *SUPER* clutch when combined with the gear I had equipped, allowing me to keep refreshing my Bananergy until my health had been fully restored before heading into the next section. As mentioned, there were two of these melons, one before the section with the fiery barrels, and one before the final section where you run across water while avoiding falling barrels to reach a mine cart that takes you through a thank you message and to the end of the stage. Being able to use these melons as a means of fully restoring my health probably made the difference between winning on that attempt, and having to try again from the beginning, as otherwise, I likely would've died to the barrels in the first half of this final section.

I'm glad to have finished the DK Challenge so that it is no longer looming over my head with the deadline approaching. I really enjoyed this event, especially the Bananza side of it. These challenges have made me play the game in different ways that I wouldn't have other wise, such as tackling some of its difficult post-game challenges with no skills unlocked, making them that much harder. It makes me hope to see more of these 'challenge' events in the future for other franchises, or, better yet, see Nintendo implement a system-wide achievements system. The likelihood of that happening mid-generation is probably pretty low, but that would be the dream. A system-wide achievements system has been among my most wanted features for any Nintendo platform for a *long* time now, and the DK Challenge offered a tantalizing glimpse into what that could look like.
