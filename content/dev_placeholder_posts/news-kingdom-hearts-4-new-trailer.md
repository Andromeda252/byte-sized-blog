# Kingdom Hearts 4 is real, baby!
            
And it's coming to Nintendo Switch 2 on Day 1.

Today's Nintendo Direct featured a plethora of exciting announcements, but for me, none were bigger (yes, bigger than OOT remake) than the absolute bombshell that was the new trailer for Kingdom Hearts 4.

If you weren't aware, I'm a massive Kingdom Hearts fan, and have been highly anticipating the fourth mainline entry since it's initial announcement 4 years ago. I was praying this year's summer showcase season would give us a new glimpse at the game, but I was starting to accept we'd have to wait a while longer after no-showing at the PlayStation and Xbox shows, as well as Summer Game Fest. So, I was ill-prepared for it to show up in a Nintendo Direct of all places.

The trailer's fairly short, but includes some juicy details for fans such as myself to marinate over until more information is provided. We get a good look at the game's frenetic combat system, which seems to heavily utilize some kind of chains that Sora can create from his Keyblade as a means of both offense and traversal. We also get a glimpse at some new and familiar characters, including what appears to be our first look at Riku in the game, standing over Sora in the rain with an umbrella, as well as what is very likely Luxord, one of the members of Organization XIII. What role he may play in the story is impossible to say, but it's cool to see him after Re:Mind's true ending seemingly teased his presence in Quadratum.

No indication of a release window yet, but hopefully it won't be too much longer before we learn more!
